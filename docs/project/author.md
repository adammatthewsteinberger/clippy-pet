# About the author

<div class="cp-bubble">It looks like you want to know who's responsible for this. Fair. He'd like you to know it was on purpose.</div>

**Adam Matthew Steinberger** is a Senior Azure & AI Development Engineer at The Vizius Group in Greenville, South Carolina, and an autistic / neurodivergent engineer who says so in his headline because it's part of how he works: systematically, thoroughly, and with a preference for evidence over adjectives. That's the sensibility behind a paperclip pet that ships with blind QA reports.

He writes about how engineers can be heard without becoming salespeople. *Engineering Influence* is a field guide to earning attention honestly: hooks that deliver what they promise, awe over outrage, "honesty gets you the share", and a hard rule against manipulating the audience you're trying to serve. This documentation site is an attempt to practise that on a small, silly, well-made thing. His earlier book, *From Novice to Navigator*, is for engineers early in their careers.

He builds and maintains privacy-first, open-source software, prefers tools that don't phone home, and thinks a good disclaimer is a feature.

Clippy Pet was made to brighten the hearts of Clippy lovers and to bring the old school Digital AI Assistant into the 21st century.

## Supporting the project

Clippy Pet is free and will stay free. If it made you smile, a star on the repository helps more than you'd think (package-manager reviewers look), and a bug report helps more than a star. If a **Sponsor** button appears on the repository, that's the maintainer's, and it's optional.
