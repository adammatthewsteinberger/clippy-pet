# Community

<div class="cp-bubble">It looks like you'd like to join in. There's room; the paperclip drawer is large.</div>

Clippy Pet is a small volunteer project with a lead maintainer, a code of conduct, and a low bar for saying hello.

- **[Contributing](contributing.md)**: setup, GitFlow branches, PR checklist, writing style.
- **[Show and tell](show-and-tell.md)**: post your remix, your terminal screenshot, your pet-that-isn't-a-paperclip.
- **[Support](support.md)**: where to ask what.
- **[Security](security.md)**: how to report a vulnerability privately.
- **[Code of conduct](code-of-conduct.md)**: Contributor Covenant 2.1.
- **[Governance](governance.md)**: who decides what, and how that might change.

The quickest ways to help right now, in rough order of impact:

1. **Install it, and tell us if it didn't work** ([bug report](https://github.com/adammatthewsteinberger/clippy-pet/issues/new/choose)).
2. **Star the repo** so package-manager reviewers see it's used. Homebrew-core, for one, has a notability bar.
3. **Package it for your ecosystem**; the recipes are drafted and listed on the [package managers](../packages/managers.md) page.
4. **Improve a frame** ([submit a variant](../make/submit.md)).
5. **Fix a sentence** on any page with the pencil icon in the top right.
