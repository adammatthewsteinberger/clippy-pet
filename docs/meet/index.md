# Meet Clippy Pet

<div class="cp-hero" markdown>
<div markdown>

<div class="cp-bubble">Hi. It looks like you're meeting a paperclip. I have nine moods and sixteen ways of looking at you.</div>

Clippy Pet is a small, deliberately old-school character built to a modern spec. Below is everything it can do, why it looks the way it does, and where the idea of a helpful office paperclip came from in the first place (spoiler: 1996, and it never really left).

- **[The nine animations](animations.md)**: idle, waving, jumping, three kinds of running, failed, waiting, review, each as a looping preview.
- **[Sixteen look directions](look-directions.md)**: the eyes follow your pointer in 22.5° steps.
- **[The paperclip story](history.md)**: Office Assistant, Clippit, the 2001 send-off, and the nostalgia comeback.
- **[Design notes](design.md)**: silhouette, palette, why 192×208, and reading well at 64 pixels.
